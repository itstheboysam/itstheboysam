Who we help

Clear information helps guests take the first step.

When people choose a place to eat or stay, they want to know what kind of place it is, what is on the menu, and how to get there. Clear information helps both local customers and visiting guests choose with confidence.

Menus · hours · seats

Restaurants & cafés

Menus, opening hours, the feel of the room. We put the details that change every week into a form first-time guests can follow.

Rooms · access · seasons

Ryokan & small inns

Rooms, ways to spend the day, access and places nearby. We arrange what guests want to know before they book.

OUR SERVICES

Four ways to support your business.

From getting discovered to showing what makes you special.

01

Website creation

Welcoming, easy-to-use websites that show what makes your restaurant or inn special.

The challenge

Outdated information and pages that are difficult to read on a phone can leave guests unsure about visiting or booking.

How we help

  • Mobile-friendly page layouts
  • Clear menus, room information, directions and opening hours
  • Easy access to contact details and existing booking services
02

Google Maps improvements

Make opening hours, directions and essential business information easy to find.

The challenge

Incorrect hours, outdated details and limited photos can make it harder for people to understand your business after finding it on Google Maps.

How we help

  • Review and organize business details, hours and contact information
  • Connect website and menu links
  • Recommend clearer presentation of photos and services
03

Paid advertising

Google, Instagram and Facebook campaigns that introduce your business to relevant audiences.

The challenge

A website alone may not reach people who have not heard of your business. Campaigns need a clear purpose and a suitable budget.

How we help

  • Google, Instagram and Facebook campaign setup
  • Audience targeting based on location and relevant interests
  • Creative coordination, monitoring, optimization and reporting

Advertising spend is separate from our management fee. Costs and campaign scope are agreed in advance.

04

English menus & multilingual support

Help visiting guests understand your menu and enjoy your hospitality.

The challenge

Japanese-only information can make it difficult for international guests to understand dishes, facilities and services.

How we help

  • Readable English menu layouts
  • Clear English descriptions of food, facilities and business information
  • Wording that preserves your business’s personality
  • Additional languages discussed according to project needs

About

We start by listening.

Mise to Yado is a small studio for restaurants, cafés and ryokan in Japan. Sam and Shige work together, and we begin by hearing about your business.

Rather than produce work the way a large company does, we would like to make something that matches the scale and pace of your shop. That is why we keep to four things: the website, the menu, the map and advertising.

What matters to us is not decoration but being understood. Together we shape information that makes a local customer or a visiting guest think, “I would like to go there.”

Who you work with

Sam and Shige

Based in Tokyo.

Direct communication from initial consultation through launch and map updates.

Process

Four steps, from first conversation to launch.

Small projects follow the same steps.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    Hearing about your shop

    We hear about your business and what has been on your mind. You do not need prepared materials.

  2. 02

    Proposal & quote

    Scope · price

    We set out the pages and languages needed, then propose the content and an estimate.

  3. 03

    Creation & review

    Copy · design

    We write and design, and you review each step on the real screen.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Check after launch

    We check how the site looks once live, and tidy the map details where needed.

Scope, price and schedule are agreed with the quote and confirmed before any work begins.
